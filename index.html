I am a Senior Engineer in Performance Architecture at Samsung Semiconductor in San Jose, California. I work on our hardware performance simulator, and I study how trends in large language models (LLMs) change hardware metrics, so that those trends can drive architectural decisions for next-generation AI inference hardware.

My work spans computer architecture, memory systems, and hardware/software co-design, applied to AI inference and privacy-preserving computing.

Before Samsung, I was a Scientist at Duality Technologies (2024–2026). There I designed and implemented components of TREBUCHET, a $15M DARPA DPRIVE effort (2021–2025) that built a custom 12 nm fully homomorphic encryption (FHE) accelerator ASIC for encrypted machine and deep learning, and I was Technical Lead for ARPA-H SHARE (Sharing Data for Rapid Collaboration on Encrypted Data), a privacy-preserving platform for healthcare collaboration built with ICF and Dana-Farber Cancer Institute.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Utah in 2024, advised by Prof. Rajeev Balasubramonian in the Utah Arch Research Group. My thesis was on accelerating modern cryptography, with a focus on data movement. During my Ph.D. I was a research co-op at NVIDIA Research (2022) and AMD Research (2020). I received my B.Tech. from IIT Gandhinagar in 2017.

  2. Jul 2024 – May 2026

    Scientist

    Duality Technologies

    TREBUCHET: 12 nm FHE accelerator ASIC · DARPA DPRIVE

    A $15M DARPA effort (Apr 2021 – Nov 2025, completed) to build a custom 12 nm ASIC for homomorphic machine and deep learning, carried to a fully verified RTL implementation and top-level floorplan (176 mm², 1 GHz timing closure). The design includes a custom ISA, a microcode scheduler, and a node-array architecture, and was validated on encrypted CNN training and inference and AES transciphering.

    My work: microcode generators for FHE kernels, kernel-to-hardware mapping, dataflow optimization, and a KeySwitching dataflow that improves data reuse; I also managed the technical collaboration with the NYU subcontractor team led by Prof. Brandon Reagen on simulator development.

    Paper: “Accelerating FHE to Enable Privacy-Preserving Deep Learning,” GOMACTech 2025.

    ARPA-H SHARE: Sharing Data for Rapid Collaboration on Encrypted Data

    ARPA-H award to Duality Technologies (Scalable Solutions Office; up to $6.3M; started September 2024).

    My role: Technical Lead. Privacy-preserving healthcare analytics across distributed patient-data owners, combining FHE, federated learning, differential privacy, and post-quantum cryptography, with ICF and Dana-Farber Cancer Institute.
